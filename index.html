Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does Golf Side Games require an internet connection or account? No. Everything runs 100% offline on your iPhone with zero sign-up required.
  • How do I restore my Pro subscription or Pro Lifetime purchase? Open the Settings tab inside the app and tap Restore Purchases.
  • How do I cancel or manage my Pro Annual subscription? Open your iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Golf Side Games.
  • Does the app process or transfer real money? No. Golf Side Games is strictly an offline scoring and friendly points-tally calculator.
