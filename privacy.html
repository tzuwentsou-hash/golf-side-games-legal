Golf Side Games: Skins, Nassau

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Short version: Golf Side Games works 100% offline, requires no account, and collects zero personal data. Everything you score stays on your device.

1. No Account & No Data Collection

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or share an email address to use Golf Side Games. We do not collect, store, transmit, rent, or sell your personal information.

The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no cross-app tracking technologies.

2. On-Device Storage & Optional iCloud Sync

Your rounds, player names, course handicaps, scores, custom course templates, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework.

If iCloud backup or private iCloud sync is enabled in your Apple device settings, your data may be synced privately within your own Apple iCloud account. The developer has no access to your iCloud container or your stored rounds.

3. Shareable 19th Hole Cards

When you choose to share a 19th Hole summary card, the image is rendered locally on your device and handed to the standard iOS Share Sheet. Nothing is uploaded to any developer server.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Pro Annual subscriptions and Pro Lifetime purchases are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment method, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles purchase records.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Golf Side Games collects no personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at eztsou@gmail.com.
